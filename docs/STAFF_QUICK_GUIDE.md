# DefectRAG — Staff quick guide

DefectRAG checks product photos and keeps a record of reviews. It helps find
unusual appearance. Follow workplace quality rules before accepting or rejecting
a product; the app does not replace those rules.

## Learn by doing

On **Overview**, select **Tutorial → Start guided practice**. Each page highlights
an action. Complete it to move to the next step automatically: choose a product,
add the sample, inspect, compare images, ask a question, find saved work, practise
Team review and track a corrective action through closure. Batch inspection,
alerts and public-sharing consent are included.

Use **Previous step** to repeat an action, or **Skip tutorial** to leave. The final
**Finish tutorial** returns to normal work. The practice uses a saved public
example and fictional assignments. No sign-in, new AI run, real save, Team share,
case, approval, public publishing or email is performed. Temporary practice data
is discarded on exit or page reload. Existing photo drafts and saved work remain
separate. Normal signed-in inspections save to Personal Workspace automatically;
the lesson opens history to teach the save location and privacy choice.

The invitation appears on the first Overview visit for this version in each
browser. Finishing or skipping remembers only that browser's choice. Replay it
from Overview whenever needed. Account verification is still required for real
Staff work; completing practice never grants access.

**Corrective Actions** is directly below **Review alerts** in the sidebar. It can
be opened before any inspection; protected records still require approved Staff
access and the security-code check. Reload an already-open older online page once
with **Ctrl+Shift+R** after a new dashboard release.

## Sign in

1. Open **Staff workspace** and enter the Staff email and password.
2. When asked, open the authenticator app and enter its latest six-digit security
   code. Codes change regularly. Never share a password, code or setup QR code.
3. New Staff must create an account, confirm the email, sign in and choose
   **Request staff access**. A Developer must approve the request before Staff
   work is available.

If the authenticator is unavailable, contact the workspace administrator. A
password reset does not remove the extra security-code check.

## Check photos

- **Image inspection** checks one photo and opens its result and assistant.
- **Batch inspection** checks up to 20 photos together under a new batch name.
  These photos do not need to be inspected first. It does not watch a live camera.

Choose the matching product, add clear photos and select **Run inspection** or
**Inspect batch**. Use JPEG, PNG or WebP photos, up to 10 MB each. A batch must
have a name not already used in the current workspace, and a total upload of
20 MB or less. If a batch stops, use **Continue unfinished photos** in the same
browser and account; completed photos are kept.

## Understand the result

- **Looks normal:** no unusual appearance was flagged. It is not a guarantee that
  the product has no defect.
- **Unusual appearance:** the photo differs from normal examples. Check it.
- **Needs a closer look:** the result is near the review limit. Check it carefully.

Use **Show original**, **Show highlights** and **Compare images** to inspect the
photo. Highlighted areas show differences, not a named defect or its seriousness.
The difference score is not an accuracy percentage or the chance of a defect.

The **Evidence Assistant** explains saved results and supporting references.
It does not examine the photo itself. Open references and check important advice.

## Know where work is saved

| Place | Who can see it? | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal Workspace | The signed-in account | Confirm that changes are saved online before using another device. |
| Team Workspace | Approved Staff with the required sign-in check | A separate copy appears only after **Share with team** is chosen. |
| Community gallery | Anyone | Publishing is optional. Check that the photo may be shared publicly. |
| Demo on this computer | Local app users with access to that computer | It is saved only here, outside an online account. |
| Demo in the online app | This browser session | Temporary results may disappear when the online app restarts. |

To open private work elsewhere, sign in with the **same account** and complete
its security-code check on the other device. Nothing moves into Team Workspace
automatically.

**Waiting to be confirmed online** means a change has not finished saving online.
Keep the app open and connected until it finishes. **Up to date** means the last
check completed. If work changed in two places, choose **Compare saved changes**;
both copies are kept until a choice is made. Do not assume unsaved changes will
be available after this computer is switched off.

**Copy older work into this account** lets approved Staff choose older local-only
inspections. Check that the work belongs in the selected account before copying.
Originals stay on the computer and nothing is shared with the team.

## Review shared work

1. Open **Inspection history**, choose **Team Workspace**, then **Review**.
2. Check the photo, result and any shared conversation.
3. Under **Staff review**, choose the status and review decision, add a reason
   if helpful, and choose **Save review**.
4. Use **Team notes** for extra collaboration. A Staff decision does not change
   the original automatic inspection result.

**Mark as seen** only records that a review alert was noticed. It does not accept
the product, complete a Staff review or prove that an email was delivered.

## Follow up a confirmed defect

1. Open **Corrective Actions** in the sidebar. Under **Create and assign a case**,
   choose a **Team inspection** from the list. Personal inspections must first be
   explicitly shared with the team.
2. Choose **Open photo and Staff review**. Check the evidence and save a Staff
   review as **Reviewed** and **Reject** when a defect is confirmed. Assignment
   fields can be drafted beforehand; only case creation waits for a valid review.
   Keep an appropriate **Accept** or **Hold** decision unchanged. Alternatively, open a shared
   inspection from Team history and choose **Create or view corrective actions**.
3. Describe what needs fixing, assign the person responsible and supervisor,
   choose the priority and due date, then confirm that a Staff review found a
   defect needing follow-up. Choose **Create corrective action**.
4. Open **Corrective Actions → Details** to view progress and history.
5. The person responsible, supervisor or a Developer can save permitted status,
   assignment and due-date changes with a note. Other approved Staff can read the
   case. Complete the security-code check when asked.

An unusual automatic result alone cannot create a case. A saved case does not
mean that an email has been sent. Deadlines use the device's time zone and must be
within the next year. The existing n8n workflow targets the assigned supervisor,
not automatically the person responsible. Corrective Action production delivery
remains inactive pending hosting and activation; arrange the handover separately.
Developer signup-request emails use a separate service. A review that already has
a case offers **Open existing case**, preserving its assignment and history.

Drafts are not saved records. They stay on this page until submission and are lost
on reload, sign-out or loss of verified access. Normal background refresh keeps the draft. Selecting a
different inspection clears that inspection's draft to avoid assigning the wrong
product. A newly saved review keeps the assignment text but requires confirmation
again. The message beside **Create corrective action** explains any missing step.
If another person changes an open case while an update is being drafted, the draft
is kept and saving is blocked. Copy any text needed, then choose **Load latest case
and replace draft** before continuing. Closed and cancelled cases are read-only.

## Recover work or get help

**Archive history** moves matching inspections out of the active list; it does
not delete the saved photos and results. Use **History recovery** for work this
account may restore. Ask a Developer to restore Team work. Temporary demo storage
can still be lost when the online app restarts.

Read the message beside a disabled action. It explains whether to add a photo,
finish sign-in, enter a security code, wait for approval or retry the connection.
For continued problems, contact the workspace administrator. Do not send passwords
or security codes. **Help & references** has the in-app guide; technical details
are available there separately.

## Small-screen navigation and saved results

On a phone, swipe the menu sideways to find more pages, including Corrective Actions.
Opening a saved inspection moves to its result. Opening a public gallery preview
moves to that preview; **Close preview** returns to the same row. Tables can scroll
sideways when all columns do not fit.

**Batch history** shows inspections with a batch label. **Inspection history**
shows individual and batch inspections. A demo review is a temporary practice
decision in that browser, never an official Team review or corrective action.
While a photo or answer is being prepared, finish that task before choosing a new
photo or starting a new inspection. Browsing other pages does not cancel the work.
