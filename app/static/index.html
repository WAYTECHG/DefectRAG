FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What the result means

Is the anomaly score a probability?+

No. It is a feature-distance score measured against normal reference images. The displayed threshold is a benchmark-derived operating point.

Is the normal reference the ground truth for my upload?+

No. A public upload has no verified ground-truth label. The reference is a dataset-verified normal example that helps you compare expected appearance.

Does the bounding box prove the defect location?+

It marks the connected region around the strongest DINOv3 deviation. A reviewer should confirm whether the highlighted difference is operationally relevant.

Where do the product rules come from?+

The dataset supplies images and ground-truth masks, not a complete product manual. Developer-reviewed rules need their own traceable sources. When none are available, the report states the limit.

Why might the first inspection take longer?+

Free cloud services can sleep while idle, and shared GPU inference may queue or need to load models before processing. The timer shows actual elapsed time, while the workflow illustration is not a live server trace.

Can this replace a certified inspection process?+

No. It is decision-support software. Production acceptance requires validated thresholds, approved procedures, representative data, and qualified human oversight.
